Architecture

How FinShield Works

Four independent analysis streams fused through reliability-weighted noisy-OR aggregation.

Document Forgery Detection

Dual-branch EfficientNet-B0 spatial + DCT frequency-domain CNN. Detects pixel-level tampering, splicing, and digital forgery artifacts.

97.92% Acc 224x224 input 1,088 samples

Video Deepfake Analysis

ResNeXt-50 frame encoder + LSTM temporal modelling with gated rich attention. TTA horizontal flip + tuned threshold.

~80% Acc 32 frames 112x112 faces

Biometric Face Match

YuNet face detection + SFace recognition. Cosine similarity between document face and video selfie for identity confirmation.

0.363 threshold 128-d embedding

Reliability-Weighted Fusion Engine

Per-modality probabilities are adjusted by reliability weights (p' = 0.5 + r*(p - 0.5)), then combined via noisy-OR: risk = 1 - product(1 - p'). Hard vetoes override fusion: face mismatch triggers rejection, insufficient evidence escalates to review. Thresholds: Approve <35%, Review 35-65%, Reject >65%.
